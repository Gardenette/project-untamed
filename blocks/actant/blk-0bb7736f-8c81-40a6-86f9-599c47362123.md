## Personality

Professor Gregory isn't the kind of person you'd typically expect to work in a lab. In fact, if you didn't know him, you'd think he spends his days herding cattle and his nights strumming his guitar by the campfire and sleeping under the stars. Nevertheless, he's a steadfast presence in the lab, and one would be foolish to mistake his lax stride and terse conversation for lack of intelligence.

### Mannerisms

Three ways to keep the Bookshelf organized:

- Spine color. Each notebook has a colored spine on the shelf. This one is blue.
- Labels. Notebooks can carry labels — this one has "how-to" and "demo" — and the shelf can filter by them.
- Backing. A notebook can be marked as backing research for another block. This notebook backs the John Doe entry: open John Doe in Worldbuilding to see it listed there.

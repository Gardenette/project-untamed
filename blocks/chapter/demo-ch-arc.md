This chapter covers how to manage the big-picture structures in a story using Foma's Story Arc tooling.

In this chapter, try:

* [ ] Type `/storyarc` or `/map` (Ctrl+Shift+M) to open the Story Arc view. Alternatively, you may click the Story Arc icon next to the Manuscript domain in the side bar.

![image-11.png](assets/image-11.png)

* [ ] Create a new *Section* within the Story Arc view.
* [ ] Create a new *Subsection* inside of a *Section*.
* [ ] Drag a scene from one *Section&#x20;*&#x74;o another, then back.
* [ ] Drag a scene from the *unassigned* zone to a *Section*.
* [ ] In the side bar, with Structure Mode toggled *on*, drag a scene into a new order, then double-click a title to rename it.

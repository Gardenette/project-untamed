*Sections* and *Subsections* created in the Story Arc view can be exposed in the side bar by toggling the `Structure Mode` view. Click the button next to the *Manuscript&#x20;*&#x64;omain in the side bar to expose the structure that you defined in the Story Arc view.

![image-32.png](assets/image-32.png)

Structure Mode reveals the *Sections* and *Subsections* that were created in the Story Arc view, though functionality is limited. You can still re-color, rename, and re-order *Sections*. You can still create new *Sections*. However, you can only edit the active layout from the side bar. To toggle between layouts, please do so from the Story Arc view.

## Structure Mode Tools

While in structure mode, create a new *Section* by clicking `New Section` at the bottom of the Manuscript domain in the side bar.

![image-35.png](assets/image-35.png)

To reveal other Structure Mode actions, hover over an existing *Section* or *Subsection* in the side bar to reveal its action icons.

![image-36.png](assets/image-36.png)

Actions include: Add new *Subsection*, Delete *Section*, Re-color *Section*, or drag the *Section*'s handle to reorder the *Section*.

The Chevron on the left of the *Section* will collapse all chapters and scenes that are contained in the *Section*.

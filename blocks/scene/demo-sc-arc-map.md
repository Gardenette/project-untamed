The Story Arc view presents a bird's-eye view of the story. It helps writers visually map out the beats and pacing of their story, exposing its skeleton.

Open the Story Arc view by typing `/storyarc` or `/map` in the editor or by clicking the 'map' icon next to the Manuscript domain in the side bar.

![image-14.png](assets/image-14.png)

The Story Arc view can be opened in its own window by clicking the 'pop out' button in the upper-right corner of the story arc view.

![image-13.png](assets/image-13.png)

## Layouts

Foma includes several default *Layouts&#x20;*&#x74;hat represent common organizational structures. By default, no layout is selected. To select a layout, click on the layout button in the upper-left corner of the screen (in this demo project, a layout has already been created for you called *Demo Tour).*

![image-12.png](assets/image-12.png)

### Templates

Common story layouts can be configured from Templates. Foma offers the following layouts out of the box: 3-Act Structure, 5-Act Structure, Hero's Journey, Save the Cat, or Game Structure.

To change the layout of your story, click `New from Template` and select the template that fits your story and/or workflow.

![image-16.png](assets/image-16.png)

To create your own Layout, click `+ New Blank Layout`. Once you are happy with your layout, you may save it as a template for future projects by clicking `Save as Template`.

***

## Sections

At the core, layouts are simply *Sections* and *Subsections* in which you nest your chapters and scenes. To create a *Section*, click `+ Add Section` at the bottom of the Story Arc view.

![image-18.png](assets/image-18.png)

### Subsections

For more complex structures, writers may find that they require further segmentation of their chapters and scenes. For example, Kaytastic's 27 chapter novel outline divides a story into 3 Acts, and each act is further subdivided into 3 units of 3 chapters each.

To add *Subsections* to a *Section*, click the "Folder +" button inside of a *Section*.

![image-20.png](assets/image-20.png)

Rename a *Section* or *Subsection* by double-clicking its title, typing, then pressing `Enter` to confirm.

## Arranging Chapters and Scenes

Chapters can be added to a Layout by click-dragging the handle of the chapter in the "Unassigned" *Section* to the right of the layout display and placing it inside of the desired *Section* or *Subsection*.

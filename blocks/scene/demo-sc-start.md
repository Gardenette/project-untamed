This is a real, editable project. Nothing in it is precious: every "Open Demo" click creates a fresh copy, so change anything, break anything, and open a new copy whenever you like.

Each chapter teaches one feature area:

1. **Linking & Worldbuilding** — clickable names, aliases, and worldbuilding entries.

2. **Threads & Pins** — track a planted detail through to its payoff.

3. **Story Arc & Structure** — see the whole draft and rearrange it.

4. **Workflow Tools&#x20;**— `/dump`, `/sift`, Focus Mode, sprints, and Writing Stats.

5. **Drafting & Versioning&#x20;**— auto-save, cloud backup, versioning, and review features.

Read top to bottom or jump to whatever interests you. Each chapter carries its own notes with a short "try this" list — click the chapter title in the side bar to keep track of your checklists.

Two more places to look:

* The Demo checklist (`Worldbuilding → Story Notes`) tracks every exercise in one list.

* The Notebook Shelf (type `/notebooks`) holds a short notebook about notebooks, including a page of features this demo does not cover.

Three tools answer the three questions every writer eventually asks: *How is the writing actually going?* *Where did I write that one thing?* And *what else can Foma do?*

***

### Writing Stats

Foma tracks your writing sessions automatically. Sit down, write, and the record keeps itself. Open `Writing Stats` from the bar-chart icon in the upper-right corner of the title bar to see it.

![image-123.png](assets/image-123.png)

Open Writing Stats

Across the top you will find your vital signs: `Writing Streak` (consecutive days written, with your best run remembered), `Goal Streak`, `Words (net)`, `Time Written`, and `Days Active`. Below them, an activity heatmap shows every day you wrote, switchable between `week`, `month`, and `year` views. Click any day to open its sittings — which projects you worked on, for how long, and what it netted you.

Two rows of filters shape everything on the page: the `All Projects` / `This Project` toggle scopes the numbers, and the `Include:` filter counts `Manuscript` work, `Worldbuilding` work, or both — because some days the real writing is a character sheet.

Then there are goals. Click `Add Goal` to set one: **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Per Sitting**, or **Project**, measured in words, minutes, or sittings. A Project goal takes a target word count and an optional deadline, and Foma computes what that means per day in the background. Hit your daily goal and the `Goal Streak` tile starts counting.

💬

*NOTE: Stats measure net words — delete a paragraph and the day's count honestly goes down. The streaks do not judge; they just count the days you showed up.*

Try this: type `/sprint`, write for a few minutes, then open Writing Stats and find the sitting you just logged.

### Project-Wide Search

Press `Ctrl+F` or type `/search` to open the search bar. What makes it worth a section of its own is the scope picker: search the `Current document`, the `Current chapter`, or any combination of `Manuscript`, `Worldbuilding`, `Threads`, and `Notebooks` — all four together is a whole-project search.

Results group by document, and titles match too, so searching a character's name surfaces their worldbuilding page alongside every scene they walk through. Press `Enter` and `Shift+Enter` to walk forward and backward through the hits; select one to jump straight to the passage.

Try this: search for *umbrella* across the whole project. You will find it in scenes, in the Worldbuilding entry, and in the *Thread* that tracks it — three domains, one search.

### The Guide

This tutorial is not the only means of finding helpful material. Should you find that you still need to reference documentation while you're writing your own project, the `Foma Guide` can assist you. Type `/guide` (or `/help`), or click the question-mark icon in the title bar, to open it: every feature in Foma on one card wall, organized into `Writing`, `Worldbuilding`, `Planning`, `Tools`, and `Version Control`. Each card carries a plain-language description plus the exact shortcuts and slash commands that summon the feature.

![image-121.png](assets/image-121.png)

Open the Foma guide

You may find that you want the Foma Guide's reference material while you're editing rather than having to open the menu every time. If that is you, you can toggle "Enable helpful tooltips" on so that you can hover buttons throughout Foma and their guide entries surface as rich tooltips, right where you are.

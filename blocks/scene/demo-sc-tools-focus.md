Focus Mode removes distractions and allows the writer to focus on only the words on the page (or even the specific paragraph that they're writing).

***

### Entering Focus Mode

Enter Focus mode in one of three ways:

1. Click the "Focus Mode" toggle in the upper-right corner of the title bar.

![image-42.png](assets/image-42.png)

2. Type `/focus` in the editor and select `Focus Mode` by clicking the option or pressing Enter.

![image-43.png](assets/image-43.png)

3. Press `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `F`

### Exiting Focus Mode

1. Press `Esc` , or:

2. While in Focus Mode, hover the mouse near the bottom of the screen. This will open a Focus Mode Options menu. Press the `x` button to exit.

![image-44.png](assets/image-44.png)

## Focus Mode Features

Focus Mode contains several tools to help writers focus on writing and compartmentalize writing tasks.

***

### Typewriter Positioning

By default, Focus Mode uses Typewriter Positioning to ensure that the caret never drops below the writer's eye level. As you type in Focus mode, each new line pushes previous content up in the Editor. Your eyes only ever need to move left-to-right, never up or down while you type.

***

### Eye-Level Positioning

Position the caret's vertical orientation relative to your screen's size and orientation: while in Focus Mode, hover the mouse to the right edge of the screen. This reveals a vertical drag handle that you can drag to align the line that you're typing with where your eyes naturally rest on your monitor.

![image-45.png](assets/image-45.png)

***

### Page Width

To adjust the size of the page in Focus Mode, hover the cursor towards the bottom of the screen. Three sizes will be revealed: `N` (narrow), `M` (medium), `W` (wide). Your selection persists across sessions and projects.

![image-46.png](assets/image-46.png)

***

### Writing Sprints

Foma is equipped with a writing sprint timer that kicks off in Focus Mode. Begin a writing sprints in two ways:

1. While in Focus Mode, hover the cursor towards the bottom of the screen to reveal Focus Mode options and click the Stopwatch. This reveals a time selection.

![image-47.png](assets/image-47.png)

Choose your desired time increment. Then, click the "play" icon to start the timer.

![image-49.png](assets/image-49.png)

2. Type `/sprint` and press Enter.

![image-50.png](assets/image-50.png)

Then, type a time increment and press Enter:

![image-51.png](assets/image-51.png)

If `/sprint` was invoked while the editor is not in Focus Mode, entering a time interval and pressing Enter will automatically enter Focus Mode with a timer set to that interval. The timer will begin on the first keystroke.

Alternatively, type `/sprint<n>` where `n` is the desired time interval. For example, `/sprint20` will enter Focus Mode and queue a 20-minute writing sprint that begins on the first keystroke.

![image-52.png](assets/image-52.png)

### Timers & Word Counts

The sprint timer appears in the top-center of the screen. Sprint word counts appear in the top-right next to the total word count of the current document.
